Manufacturing

Manufacturing Capabilities

A digital-drawing workflow, in-house patternmaking and CNC machining under one roof in Noida — so moulds and variables come off the machine to your drawing, and repeat orders match the first shipment.

One roof, drawing to delivery

Everything the Part Needs, in One Place

KVG runs the full chain that a glass mould or IS-machine variable passes through — reading the drawing, making the pattern, scheduling the job, turning and milling the metal, and getting it to your plant — inside a single Noida (NCR) facility. Keeping those steps together is what removes the handoffs where accuracy and lead time usually leak away.

Our engineers read and edit digital drawings directly, so you can order against your own cavity data and part references rather than re-describing a component in an email. Patterns are made in-house instead of being subcontracted, production is planned on a computerized schedule so jobs are sequenced rather than improvised, and machining holds tolerance part after part. Behind the machining sit established foundry relationships for casting in cast iron, SG iron, aluminium and Minox, and global shipping that keeps export handling straightforward for plants worldwide.

The result is the loop the rest of the market leaves open: send us a drawing, and the same team that reads it makes the pattern, cuts the metal and ships the part — traceable to that drawing every step of the way.

Materials

Materials & Typical Applications

We machine four families of material and choose between them job by job. The properties below are the general reasons a plant picks each one — the final call is made against your container, campaign and machine.

Materials machined by KVG and where each typically fits
Material Why it is chosen Typical applications
Cast Iron (CI) Good thermal conductivity and a stable, cost-effective baseline for high-volume work. General blank and blow moulds, mould equipment on long, high-output runs.
SG Iron Higher strength and better resistance to thermal fatigue than grey CI. Blow moulds and heavier ware, longer campaigns where crack resistance matters.
Aluminium High thermal conductivity and light weight for fast, even heat extraction. Wide-mouth and food-jar moulds, and variables where rapid cooling helps ware quality.
Minox Selected for wear and corrosion resistance on high-duty contact surfaces. High-wear variables such as plungers and neck-ring components on extended campaigns.

Specifications shown are representative — confirm against KVG engineering drawings.
