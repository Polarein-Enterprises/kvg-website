Guala & Nip Cap Guala Neck Rings

A precise, repeatable sealing finish — built to run campaign after campaign without a leak.

Guala and nip cap Guala neck rings form the sealing finish of the container on the blank side of the IS machine. It is the one surface the closure has to seat against perfectly, so its profile, concentricity and thread form decide whether a roll-on pilfer-proof (ROPP) or Guala-type closure seals first time — a job that matters most on spirits and premium beverage lines, where the finish is the anti-tamper feature the consumer inspects.

KVG machines Guala neck rings, and the nip cap Guala variant with its integral cap-forming detail, directly from your finish drawing. Because we read and edit digital drawings in-house, we cut to your exact profile and hold it across the set, so a KVG neck ring is interchangeable with your industry-standard equipment — it drops into your existing neck ring holders and lock rings with no change to section timing or handling. That low switching cost is the point: you qualify one supplier, not a new process.

Every set is built to standard IS-machine centre distances from 3″ to 6¼″ in single, double, triple and quadruple gob, in quick-change (QC) and non-QC forms. Material — CI, SG iron, Aluminium or Minox — is chosen for the finish detail, the ware weight and the campaign length you run, then finished and hardened for wear at the sealing surface. The result is consistent ware quality, fewer rejects at the closure and a longer service life between changes.

Why KVG

Engineered Where the Seal Is Made

  • Machined to your finish drawing — digital drawings read and edited in-house, so the thread and sealing profile match your closure exactly and seat first time.
  • Interchangeable with industry-standard neck rings — drops into your existing holders and lock rings with no change to section timing, keeping switching cost at zero.
  • Held concentric across the set — matched halves on CNC turning centres keep the bore true, so the closure applies evenly and pilfer-proof bands break as intended.
  • Nip cap detail formed integral — the cap-forming feature is cut into the ring rather than added later, removing an assembly part and a leak path.
  • Material matched to the campaign — CI, SG iron, Aluminium or Minox chosen for finish detail and run length, then hardened at the sealing surface for wear life.
  • QC and non-QC to suit your changeovers — quick-change builds cut downtime on frequent job changes; non-QC suits long, stable campaigns.
  • Full SG–QG range, one supplier — single through quadruple gob from 3″ to 6¼″ centres, so you qualify one source for the whole neck-ring set.
