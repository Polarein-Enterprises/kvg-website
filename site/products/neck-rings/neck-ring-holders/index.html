Neck Ring Holders

Carry the finish true — open, close and release without wandering the seal.

The neck ring holder is the working half of the closure mechanism on the blank side of the IS machine. It carries the two neck ring halves on the arm, closes them around the parison to form the finish, then opens to release finished ware to the take-out. Every cycle depends on the holder locating the rings in exactly the same place, because the moment the halves close off-centre the finish loses concentricity and the closure stops sealing cleanly.

KVG machines neck ring holders as a matched pair from your drawing, with true location faces and hinge geometry that keep the rings aligned cycle after cycle. Because holders are cut to standard IS-machine centre distances, a KVG holder is interchangeable with your industry-standard mechanism — it mounts on your existing arm, carries your current neck ring halves and lock rings, and needs no change to section timing. You replace a worn holder without re-qualifying the whole closure system.

Holders are supplied in single, double, triple and quadruple gob across the 3″ to 6¼″ centre range, in quick-change (QC) and non-QC forms. Material — CI, SG iron, Aluminium or Minox — is chosen for the thermal load and campaign length the holder sees close to the parison, then finished for wear at the location and pivot faces. The outcome is a holder that opens and closes cleanly, holds the finish concentric and lasts longer between rebuilds — fewer stops, steadier ware quality.

Why KVG

Built to Locate, Cycle and Last

  • Matched-pair machining — both halves cut together on CNC turning and milling centres, so location faces meet true and the neck rings close concentric.
  • Interchangeable with industry-standard holders — mounts on your existing arm and carries your current neck rings and lock rings, so a replacement needs no re-qualification.
  • Clean open-and-close action — hinge and pivot geometry cut to drawing keeps the release crisp, protecting the finish as ware transfers to take-out.
  • Wear-finished location faces — the surfaces that set concentricity are hardened, so alignment holds through the campaign, not just the first shift.
  • Material matched to thermal load — CI, SG iron, Aluminium or Minox selected for the heat the holder sees near the parison and the run length you plan.
  • QC and non-QC builds — quick-change holders cut downtime on frequent job changes; non-QC holders suit long, stable production.
  • Full SG–QG range from one source — single through quadruple gob from 3″ to 6¼″ centres, matched to your neck rings for a single point of supply.
