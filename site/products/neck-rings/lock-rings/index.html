Lock Rings

Hold the clamp true — the small ring that keeps the finish repeatable.

The lock ring is the part that keeps the closure system honest. It clamps the two neck ring halves into the neck ring holder and sets the load that holds them concentric while the finish is formed. It is a small component with an outsized effect: if the lock ring is worn or out of round, the halves shift, the finish loses concentricity and the closure stops seating — so a true, wear-resistant lock ring is the cheapest insurance a blank side can carry.

KVG machines lock rings to your drawing and to standard IS-machine centre distances, so a KVG lock ring is interchangeable with your industry-standard equipment. It clamps your existing neck ring halves into your current holders with no change to the mechanism or the section timing, which means you can replace a worn ring as routine maintenance rather than a project. Because the lock ring is a frequent-wear item, keeping a KVG set on the shelf is a simple way to protect finish quality between rebuilds.

Lock rings are supplied in quick-change (QC) and non-QC builds across the 3″ to 6¼″ centre range, matched to single, double, triple and quadruple gob layouts. Material — CI, SG iron, Aluminium or Minox — is selected for the clamping load and the wear the ring sees each cycle, then finished and hardened at the clamping face so the load stays true across the campaign. The result is a lock ring that holds the neck rings concentric, keeps the finish repeatable and reduces closure rejects.

Why KVG

The Small Part That Protects the Finish

  • True, round clamping face — machined on CNC turning centres so the load sits evenly on the neck ring halves and holds them concentric.
  • Interchangeable with industry-standard lock rings — clamps your existing neck rings into your current holders with no mechanism change, so replacement is routine maintenance.
  • Hardened at the wear face — the clamping surface is finished for wear, so the clamp load stays true across the campaign instead of drifting as the ring beds in.
  • Material matched to the load — CI, SG iron, Aluminium or Minox chosen for clamping load and cycle wear, balancing weight against service life.
  • QC and non-QC builds — quick-change lock rings speed neck-ring changeovers; non-QC rings suit long, stable campaigns.
  • Segmented option for larger finishes — segmented lock rings ease handling and even clamping on wider centre distances.
  • Stock-friendly wear item — a low-cost part that protects finish quality, so a KVG set on the shelf keeps the line running between rebuilds.
