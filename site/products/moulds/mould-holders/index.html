Moulds & Mould Equipment

Mould Holders

Rigid, well-cooled holders that keep mould halves in register — job after job, campaign after campaign.

A precise mould only performs if what carries it is equally precise. Mould holders are the arms that clamp the blank and blow moulds onto the Individual Section (IS) machine, hold both halves in accurate register, and deliver cooling wind to the mould faces. Get the holder wrong and even the best cavity produces flash, uneven walls and finish defects.

KVG mould holders are engineered for rigidity first. Stiff arms and hard-wearing pivot and clamp faces keep the parting line tight, so the two mould halves close on the same plane every cycle — that is what suppresses flash and keeps wall thickness even. Holders are built for both the blank side and the blow side, to standard IS-machine centre distances from 3″ to 6¼″ and for single through quadruple gob operation, and they follow industry-standard mounting geometry so they fit the section hardware you already run. That interchangeability keeps switching cost low and lets you standardise holders across machines.

Cooling is designed in, not bolted on. Integrated wind and plenum passages carry cooling air evenly to the mould, holding a stable mould temperature that extends campaign life and protects ware quality. Quick-change interfaces cut the time a section stands idle during a job change, returning that time to production. Holders are machined from cast iron, SG (ductile) iron or aluminium — weight and stiffness matched to the section — and, where required, Minox for corrosion resistance. We build to your drawing and centre distance so registration is correct the first time, and every holder is traceable to its part reference for repeat orders.

Why these holders

Engineered Features, Practical Outcomes

  • Rigid alignment of mould halves — a tight, consistent parting line that suppresses flash and finish defects.
  • Integrated wind and plenum cooling — even mould temperature across the campaign, protecting ware quality and extending mould life.
  • Quick-change interfaces — faster job changes that return idle section time to production.
  • Interchangeable with standard mounts — fits your existing section hardware, keeping switching cost low.
  • CI, SG Iron or Aluminium build — weight and stiffness matched to the section, with Minox available for corrosion resistance.
  • Hard-wearing pivot and clamp faces — sustained accuracy and clamp force over long campaigns.
  • Built to your drawing and centre distance — correct registration first time, with full part-reference traceability for repeats.
