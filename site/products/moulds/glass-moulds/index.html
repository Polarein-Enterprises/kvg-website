Glass Moulds (Blank & Blow)

Section-matched blank and blow moulds engineered for repeatable ware and long campaign life.

The blank and blow moulds are the heart of the container-forming set, and KVG makes them as a matched pair rather than two unrelated castings. The blank mould shapes the parison; the blow mould finishes the container. When both halves are cut to the same drawing, on the same machines, glass distribution stays consistent from the settle-blow to the final blow — which is what keeps wall thickness even and reject rates low.

Every set is engineered for standard Individual Section (IS) machine centre distances from 3″ to 6¼″ and for single, double, triple and quadruple gob operation. Because the mounting interfaces follow industry-standard geometry, KVG moulds are interchangeable with the mould equipment already running on your line: they drop into existing holders without re-tooling the section, so switching supplier does not mean switching hardware. That low switching cost is deliberate — it lets a plant trial KVG on one cavity before committing a full job.

Moulds are machined from cast iron, SG (ductile) iron, aluminium or Minox, with the material chosen to the container weight, campaign length and thermal load rather than to a house default. Venting and cooling geometry is worked into the cavity so heat leaves the glass in a controlled way, extending campaign life and protecting ware quality. Our engineers read and edit digital drawings in-house, so if you already have cavity data we can work directly from it, and every mould is traceable to its drawing and part reference so repeat orders match the first shipment exactly.

Why these moulds

Engineered Features, Practical Outcomes

  • Matched blank/blow pairing — consistent glass distribution across the forming cycle for stable wall thickness and fewer rejects.
  • Interchangeable with industry-standard equipment — drop-in replacement in existing holders, so switching supplier keeps switching cost and downtime low.
  • Optimised venting and cooling geometry — controlled heat transfer out of the glass for longer campaign life and better ware.
  • CI, SG Iron, Aluminium or Minox — material selected to container weight and thermal load rather than a house default.
  • Digital-drawing workflow — we read and edit your existing cavity data in-house before any metal is cut.
  • Consistent cavity finish and hardening — reduced sticking and surface checks for a cleaner container.
  • Full drawing and part-reference traceability — repeat orders match the first shipment, cavity for cavity.
