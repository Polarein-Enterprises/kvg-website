Moulds & Mould Equipment

Blanks

Sound, close-tolerance mould blanks that machine cleanly and hold their finish through the campaign.

A finished mould is only ever as good as the blank it started from. KVG supplies mould blanks — the cast and semi-finished blocks from which blank and blow cavities are cut — so the tooling on your line begins with dense, sound metal rather than a casting that hides porosity until the cavity is half machined.

Blanks are supplied at the stage that suits your shop. Take them as raw castings if you finish moulds in-house, as rough-machined blocks with a uniform machining allowance if you want to skip the heavy stock removal, or as finish-machined parison and blow blanks ready to run. All are produced to standard IS-machine centre distances from 3″ to 6¼″ and to single, double, triple and quadruple gob layouts, so a blank drops into your existing holders and section hardware without modification. That interchangeability is the point: it lets a plant standardise on one blank source across several machine sizes.

Material is matched to the job — cast iron for general narrow-neck work, SG (ductile) iron where mechanical strength matters, aluminium for lightweight and fast-cooling applications, and Minox where a corrosion-resistant, fine-grained surface is needed. KVG works through established foundry relationships for consistent metallurgy and controlled hardness, so tool life is predictable when you machine. Our in-house capability to read and edit digital drawings means we can match your parison profile straight from your data, and every batch is traceable so repeat blanks are identical to the first.

Why these blanks

Engineered Features, Practical Outcomes

  • Sound, dense castings — sourced through established foundry relationships, so no porosity surprises appear once you cut the cavity.
  • Choice of supply condition — cast, rough-machined or finish-machined, so you buy at the stage that fits your shop and skip work you do not need.
  • Consistent, controlled hardness — predictable tool life and clean cutting across CI, SG Iron, Aluminium and Minox.
  • Uniform machining allowance — generous, even stock so cavities finish cleanly without breaking through.
  • Standard centre distances and gob configs — blanks that drop into your existing holders and section hardware.
  • Digital-drawing capability — we match your parison profile directly from your data before casting or machining.
  • Batch traceability — every repeat blank is identical to the first, order after order.
