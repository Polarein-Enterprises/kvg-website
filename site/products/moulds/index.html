Moulds & Mould Equipment

Moulds are where a gob of molten glass first takes shape. On an IS (individual section) machine the blank mould forms the parison and the blow mould finishes the container, so mould quality decides wall thickness, weight distribution and surface finish on every article that leaves the line.

KVG manufactures blank and blow moulds, blanks and mould holders for standard IS-machine centre distances from 3″ to 6¼″, in single-, double-, triple- and quadruple-gob (SG/DG/TG/QG) layouts. We read and edit your digital drawings in-house and cast in cast iron, SG iron, aluminium or Minox to match the job. For plant engineers that means dimensional repeatability job after job; for buyers it means one supplier for the complete mould set, machined and delivered from Noida with short lead times.
