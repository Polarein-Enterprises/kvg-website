Blank-Side Equipment

Plungers

Controlled glass distribution from the first press.

The plunger sets the inside of the container before the blow side ever sees it. On press-and-blow it presses the gob up against the blank mould and neck ring to form the parison, fixing wall thickness and glass distribution in one stroke; on blow-and-blow it forms the finish bore and guide during settle and counter-blow. Because the plunger carries heat away from the thickest part of the glass, its cooling and its concentricity decide whether ware runs even or runs thin — and that is what KVG engineers into every plunger.

KVG plungers are made for the individual-section (IS) machine across single, double, triple and quadruple gob layouts (SG / DG / TG / QG) and standard centre distances from 3″ to 6¼″, in both quick-change (QC) and conventional (Non-QC) formats. They are dimensioned to be interchangeable with industry-standard plunger mechanisms, so a replacement drops into your existing cylinders and cooling circuit without re-work. Internal cooling is profiled to pull heat evenly from tip to base, holding the plunger straight and stabilising distribution across a long campaign — the difference between consistent fill weights and creeping reject rates.

We produce plungers in Minox, SG Iron and CI, matching the material and surface treatment to your glass, cycle rate and cooling medium, and machine each one for concentricity with its matched thimble and neck ring. Every plunger is made to your drawing — read, edited and scheduled in-house — on Jyoti CNC turning centres and BFW vertical mills at Noida, so lead times stay short and repeat orders stay identical. For a new job or a worn set, KVG plungers keep glass where the design put it.
