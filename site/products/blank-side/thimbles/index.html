Thimbles

Precise finish forming, paired to your plunger.

The thimble is the quiet half of the neck-forming set. In blow-and-blow forming it sits in the neck ring and guides the plunger, forming the finish bore and the initial guide hole while the settle and counter-blow do their work. It runs against hot glass on every cycle and must stay concentric with the plunger to the last thou — if it wanders, the finish goes oval and the whole set starts making rejects. KVG thimbles are machined as a matched partner to the plunger, so the pair works as one.

Each thimble is made for the individual-section (IS) machine in single, double, triple and quadruple gob layouts (SG / DG / TG / QG) and across standard centre distances from 3″ to 6¼″, in quick-change (QC) and conventional (Non-QC) formats. They are dimensioned to be interchangeable with industry-standard neck-forming tooling, so a replacement seats in your existing neck ring and mechanism without fitting work. We hold close tolerances on the bore and the seating diameter so the thimble locates repeatably and the finish stays round from the first bottle of a campaign to the last.

KVG produces thimbles in Minox, SG Iron and CI, choosing material and surface treatment for your glass, cycle rate and expected life, and pairs each thimble to its plunger for true concentric running. Every part is made to your drawing — read, edited and scheduled in-house — on Jyoti CNC turning centres and BFW vertical mills at Noida, keeping lead times short and repeat orders dimensionally identical. Order thimbles on their own to refresh worn tooling, or as a matched plunger-and-thimble set for a new job — either way the finish comes out right.
