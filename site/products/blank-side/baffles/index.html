Blank-Side Equipment

Baffles

Sealed settle-blow and counter-blow, cycle after cycle.

The baffle closes the top of the blank mould and directs settle-blow and counter-blow air while the parison is formed. On blow-and-blow lines it seals the cavity for both blows; on press-and-blow it forms the base of the parison as the plunger presses upward. Because it opens and closes on every machine cycle against radiant heat from the gob, the baffle lives or dies on its sealing face and its cooling — and that is exactly where KVG builds them to last.

KVG baffles are machined for the individual-section (IS) machine at standard centre distances from 3″ to 6¼″ and supplied in single, double, triple and quadruple gob layouts (SG / DG / TG / QG). They are dimensioned to be interchangeable with industry-standard baffle arms and quick-change holders, so a replacement set drops into your existing tooling without re-fixturing the section — low switching cost is the whole point. Internal air-cooling bores are laid out to hold the sealing face flat and stable through long production runs, keeping settle-blow pressure consistent and protecting parison weight.

We cut baffles from CI, SG Iron, Aluminium and Minox to suit your ware, cycle rate and cooling regime, with hardened and ground sealing faces where wear is heaviest. Every set is produced from your drawing — which our engineers read, edit and schedule in-house — on Jyoti CNC turning centres and BFW vertical mills at our Noida facility, so lead times stay short and repeat orders stay dimensionally identical. If you are standardising a plant or covering an urgent line stoppage, KVG delivers baffles that seat cleanly, seal reliably and match what is already on the machine.
