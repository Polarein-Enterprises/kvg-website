Blow-Side Equipment

Blow Heads

Designed to seal clean and deliver consistent final-blow air — so wall thickness, ware weight and finish stay repeatable shift after shift.

The blow head is the blow-side component that delivers final forming air into the blow mould, inflating the parison received from the blank side against the mould wall to form the finished container. On an IS (Individual Section) machine it drops onto and seals over the blow mould at the moment of final blow, so the quality of that seal and the consistency of air delivery govern wall-thickness distribution, ware-weight control and the soundness of the finish. A leaking or slow blow head shows up immediately as light or uneven ware and rising reject rates.

KVG blow heads are engineered to standard IS and NIS section geometry across centre distances from 3″ to 6¼″ and in single-, double-, triple- and quad-gob configurations, so one supplier covers every section on the line. They are built to interchange with industry-standard blow heads already running on your machines — a drop-in replacement that lets you qualify KVG section by section without modifying the mechanism or carrying a parallel spares inventory. The sealing face is precision-ground and hardened for a durable air-tight contact, internal passages are profiled for fast, even air delivery, and cooling-air options draw heat from the finish and shoulder zone to hold dimensions and reduce checks.

Each head is produced in the material best matched to gob weight, cycle speed and wear duty — CI, SG Iron, Aluminium or Minox — and manufactured in-house from your digital drawings on our Jyoti CNC turning centres and BFW vertical milling machines. From our Noida (NCR) facility that means short, predictable lead times on both first-fit sets and running spares.

Engineered features

Built for Uptime and Ware Quality

  • Hardened, precision-ground sealing face — holds an air-tight seal over the blow mould, keeping final-blow pressure consistent and wall distribution repeatable across the campaign.
  • Profiled internal air passages — deliver forming air quickly and evenly, shortening blow time and stabilising ware weight at production speed.
  • Cooling-air options — draw heat from the finish and shoulder zone to improve dimensional stability and reduce checks in the ware.
  • Interchangeable with industry-standard blow heads — drop-in replacement for existing sections, so you qualify KVG without modifying the mechanism.
  • Quick-change mounting — cap-head fixings in place of taper pins speed up changeovers and cut downtime at job change.
  • Material matched to duty — CI, SG Iron, Aluminium or Minox selected for gob weight, cycle speed and wear, for the longest practical service life.
  • Single- to quad-gob range — one supplier across your 3″–6¼″ sections means consistent spares, drawings and lead times.
