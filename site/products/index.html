Our Product Range

Products for the Container Glass Industry

KVG Enterprises manufactures and supplies the moulds, variables and spare parts an IS (individual section) machine needs to run — organised the way glass plants actually work, by process stage. Eleven product lines sit under four categories that follow the glass through the machine: mould equipment, the blank side, the neck ring and closure system, and the blow side.

Every line is engineered to standard IS-machine geometry — centre distances from 3″ to 6¼″, single- through quadruple-gob (SG/DG/TG/QG) layouts, QC and Non-QC variants — and produced in cast iron, SG iron, aluminium or Minox to match your job. We read and edit digital drawings in-house, so you can order to your own references and expect dimensional repeatability part after part.
