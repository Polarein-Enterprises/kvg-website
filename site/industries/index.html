Industries Served

Industries We Serve

KVG Enterprises equips the plants that make glass packaging. Our moulds, variables and spare parts run on IS (individual section) machines forming container glass — bottles and jars — and we also supply tooling for tableware glass production.

The same forming hardware serves very different end markets, and each one brings its own priorities: dimensional consistency for high-speed food and beverage lines, finish integrity for spirits, and the tightest tolerances of all for pharmaceutical containers. Below is how our product lines map to the four industries KVG supplies. Jump to a section, or send us the article you produce and we will recommend the configuration.

Industry

Food

Container types: wide-mouth jars for jams, pickles, honey, sauces and preserves; short and tall food bottles; twist-off and lug-cap finishes that demand a clean, repeatable seal.

Food packaging runs high volumes at speed, so glass distribution and finish accuracy have to hold across long production campaigns. The parts doing that work are the glass moulds and blanks that shape the article, the baffles and funnels that set gob loading on the blank side, and the neck rings and closure systems that form a lug or twist finish tight enough for vacuum sealing. We supply them in CI, SG iron and aluminium to match your job and cycle rate.

Industry

Beverage

Container types: narrow-neck bottles for water, soft drinks, juices, beer and returnable/refillable packs; crown, ROPP and pry-off finishes running at high line speeds.

Beverage is a narrow-neck, high-cavitation business where lightweighting and burst strength both matter. That puts the emphasis on the blow side and the finish: plungers and thimbles control interior forming in SG, DG, TG and QG layouts, blow heads deliver clean final-blow air and cooling for consistent wall thickness, and Guala and nip-cap neck rings form crown and ROPP finishes that seal reliably. Our tooling is built to standard IS-machine geometry so it interchanges with the equipment already on your line.

Spirits

Container types: premium liquor bottles — whisky, rum, vodka, gin and brandy — often flint or extra-flint, with heavy bases, embossed shoulders and Guala or nip-cap tamper-evident finishes.

Spirits packaging is where cosmetic quality meets closure precision: the bottle is part of the brand, so surface finish and shape definition carry weight, and the neck must accept a tamper-evident Guala fitment without leaking. Well-finished glass moulds and mould holders hold shape and parting-line quality through long runs, while Guala and nip-cap neck rings, neck ring holders and lock rings form and secure the closure the category depends on. We machine to your drawings for repeatable, on-brand results.

Pharmaceutical

Container types: moulded glass bottles and jars for syrups, tablets and dry powders; wide-mouth and narrow-neck formats with precise, defect-free finishes for child-resistant, pilfer-proof and ROPP closures.

Pharmaceutical containers carry the tightest dimensional and cosmetic tolerances of any glass we tool for — closure integrity is non-negotiable and surface defects are rejected outright. That demands tooling built and inspected to close limits: glass moulds and blanks that reproduce the article exactly, plungers for accurate interior forming, and neck rings, holders and lock rings that deliver a repeatable, leak-tight finish batch after batch. We support your quality requirements with in-house drawing control and dimensional checks.
