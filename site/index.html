Who we are

Built to Manufacture & Supply the Best

KVG Enterprises Pvt. Ltd. manufactures and supplies the variables, spares and mechanisms an IS (individual section) machine depends on to turn molten glass into finished containers, shift after shift.

From two units in the UPSIDC Surajpur estate in Greater Noida, we produce the working set: baffle, blow head, funnel and neckring arms; take-out arms, tongs and mechanisms; inserts and mould holders; and the parts that wear — shear shanks, plunger cartridges and cams. Everything is machined to your drawings and built to interchange with the equipment already on your line.

Our work is not limited to container glass. The same tooling discipline serves tableware glass producers, where dimensional accuracy and surface finish matter just as much. Across both, KVG counts leading names in the container glass industry among its customers — relationships built on parts that fit, repeat and last.

What ties it together is a plain engineering promise: read the drawing correctly, machine it accurately, and deliver it on time.
