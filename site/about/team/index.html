Team of Experts

The People Behind the Precision

A century of glass-industry know-how

Experience You Can Put on the Floor

KVG is run by engineers who have spent their careers in glass-industry tooling. Between them, the team brings more than 100 years of combined expertise, led by individuals with 40+ years of hands-on experience each in moulds, variables and IS-machine spares.

That depth shows up in the details that decide whether a part performs: how a gob loads into a blank, why a neck ring seals on one job and chatters on another, which material and hardening suit a given cavity life. Our leaders have read the drawings, cut the metal and stood by the machines — so when your enquiry arrives, it lands with people who understand the process end to end, not just the paperwork.

The result is practical problem-solving: we help specify the right configuration, flag the interchangeability that keeps switching costs low, and hold the tolerances that make parts repeat. It is the same knowledge that lets us commit to short, honest lead times — and meet them.
