About KVG Enterprises

Precision Tooling for the Container Glass Industry

Who we are

Built to Manufacture & Supply the Best

KVG Enterprises Pvt. Ltd. was incorporated to manufacture and supply top-quality moulds, variables, spare parts and allied equipment for the container glass industry — the tooling an IS (individual section) machine relies on to turn molten gob into a finished container, shift after shift.

From our state-of-the-art facility in Noida, in the National Capital Region, we produce the full working set: glass moulds and blanks, mould holders, blank-side variables such as baffles, funnels, plungers and thimbles, neck rings and closure systems, and blow-side equipment. Because these lines are made under one roof, our customers deal with a single accountable manufacturer instead of coordinating a chain of suppliers.

Our work is not limited to container glass. The same tooling discipline serves tableware glass producers, where dimensional accuracy and surface finish matter just as much. Across both, KVG counts leading names in the container glass industry among its customers — relationships built on parts that fit, repeat and last.

What ties it together is a plain engineering promise: read the drawing correctly, machine it accurately, and deliver it on time. Everything on this site — the capabilities, the process-stage catalogue, the enquiry loop — exists to make that promise easy to verify.
